Ph.D. Researcher in Artificial Intelligence

Jonathan A. Zea

Research assistant at Ecuadorian Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision Lab, Escuela Politécnica Nacional.

Currently pursuing a Ph.D. on AI Mechanistic Interpretability. My areas of interest include Deep Learning, Machine Learning, Neural Networks, Algorithms, and the intersection of AI and Philosophy. In a broader context, I am passionate about microcontrollers, embedded systems, robotics, literature, and climate change.

Biografía e Intereses

Investigador apasionado por descifrar las cajas negras del aprendizaje profundo. Mi trabajo postdoctoral y doctoral se centra en la interpretabilidad mecanicista, buscando entender cómo los modelos de redes neuronales profundas almacenan y procesan conceptos abstractos internamente.
